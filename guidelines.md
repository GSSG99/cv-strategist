# CV Best Practices & Guidelines

Add your personal CV guidelines here. Examples:

- Lead with quantified achievements (e.g. "Increased revenue by 30%")
- Use the STAR method for bullet points (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Keep the CV to 2 pages maximum
- Tailor the professional summary for each role
- Use active verbs: Led, Built, Drove, Delivered, Architected
- Include only the last 10 years of experience
- Ensure all dates are consistent (Month YYYY format)
